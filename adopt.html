The Process

How Adoption Works

Adopting through STARS and Stratford Animal Control is simple. Here's what to expect.

1

Browse Pets

Explore available animals above. Dogs, cats, and more are waiting for their forever family.

2

Apply in Advance

Submit your application through the official Town of Stratford adoption page — not through Adopt-a-Pet. Applications are reviewed by Animal Control, and applying ahead gets you priority.

3

Meet Your Match

Book a Sunday Dog Meet & Greet (1–4pm) or come to our Thursday Cat Open House (4–7pm). Our small team is often out on calls, so planning ahead guarantees someone is there to welcome you.

4

Welcome Home

Once approved, take your new best friend home and change both your lives forever.
